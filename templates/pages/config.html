⚠️ Why Protocol Stacking is ridiculous & dangerous (Click to expand diagram)

High Latency & Smaller Anonymity Set: Stacking (I2P over Tor) does not "double your privacy" — it forces your traffic through two separate overlay networks, creating huge latency penalties. More importantly, it shrinks your anonymity set down to the tiny intersection of users running both Tor AND I2P at the exact same moment. Instead of blending into a large crowd, you stand out in a tiny one while wearing a leaking, badly-stitched half-garlic half-onion costume!
